Positioning rules:
- Spatial insurance is a comparison of complementarity logic, not a directly falsified theory.
- "Species-redundant" is property-specific: multiple species independently satisfy the
  declared spatial-continuity criterion. It does not imply general functional equivalence.
- The new carrier-turnover analysis is post hoc exploratory and is not used to change
  the frozen confirmatory decision.
