Important boundaries: this is a single landscape, two comparable local-seed cohorts; interannual not seasonal contrast. Location category reflects documented position and not proof of never moved; the observed comparison is not a randomized transport effect. Missing may reflect tag loss, unrecovered seeds or fate uncertainty, and we allow broader assignment than recorded location. Germination is not establishment or lifetime plant fitness. Seed source and initial viability differ between years; original paper already reports no ex situ germinants. Novel calculation here is explicit missing-fate bounds and the separation of per-release contributions from conditional fate probabilities.
