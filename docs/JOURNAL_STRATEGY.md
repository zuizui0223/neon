# Journal strategy

## Submission order

1. **Oikos**
2. **Journal of Animal Ecology**
3. **Ecography** as spatial-pattern fallback

## Oikos story

Lead with a general ecological distinction:

> Aggregate community properties may arise by **complementarity** among species or by **redundancy** because one or more component species already carry the property.

The NEON programme tests that distinction for spatial continuity. The graph construction is measurement infrastructure, not the paper's conceptual contribution.

## Language rules

Avoid in title/abstract:
- EOG
- world survival
- canonical worlds
- claims that spatial insurance directly predicts connectivity
- claims that ORNL shows species pooling is biologically harmful

Prefer:
- prespecified trap-neighbourhood adjacency criteria
- continuity fraction
- community-minus-best-species continuity
- complementarity versus redundancy
- weakest-link effect under an all-positive continuity definition

## Endpoint translation

Historical:
"surviving-world fraction"

Paper-facing:
"proportion of prespecified trap-neighbourhood adjacency criteria satisfied"

Both refer to the same frozen calculation.
