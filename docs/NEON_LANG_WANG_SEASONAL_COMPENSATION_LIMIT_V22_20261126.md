This is **not** proof of a general seasonal effect or the relative causal benefit of rodent removal.

## Decision
The new evidence constrains compensation from the opposite side: under some recorded source-retention and predation patterns, even maximum possible post-movement survival cannot offset the loss. This is a conditional ceiling derived from the published categories, not a new field experiment, complete route-specific fate comparison, or formal meta-analysis. The key remaining requirement is genuinely independent replicated seasonal cohorts with tracked final outcomes and source/after-movement germination, rather than accumulating algebra on incomplete early fate reports. Retain V21 and all prior STOPs. No merge/submission.
