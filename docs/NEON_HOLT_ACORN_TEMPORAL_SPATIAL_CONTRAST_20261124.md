## Single ecological test

1. Fixed historical reference years 1988–1997: for each year, compute deterministic within-144-station midranks of directly observed red-oak acorn energy (ROkcal), including tied zeros. For each station calculate its mean historical rank. This is a baseline for stable **resource** spatial differences, not a station-specific animal detection calibration.
2. Fixed held-out capture years 1999–2013 (15 possible years). At each original station compute previous-label crop rank anomaly and current-label crop rank anomaly relative to the same historical reference. The current-label crop is the original upcoming-resource comparator, not necessarily available at summer capture. Do not inspect or adjust annual data outside the window. 
3. For each same original ID-year with both used and available locations, compute mean used-minus-available rank anomaly separately for preceding and upcoming crop maps. All groups weight equally within year, all years equally. Primary contrast is preceding minus upcoming. Positive preceding association AND positive preceding-minus-upcoming contrast is required for the hypothesized asymmetry; no comparison to a different outcome.
4. Effect-blind support gate: ≥10 eligible calendar years each with ≥3 complete original ID-year strata, ≥60 eligible ID-year strata across eligible years; require dates in June–August of capture year and 100% used-date/year consistency among rows in target window; no alternative months or year ranges on failure. The result includes counts of dropped groups and unmatched source keys, but does not export animal or trap IDs. Archive source STOP if gate fails.
5. Report equal-year means, number of positive years, and range of leave-one-year-out mean contrasts. The directional descriptive criterion requires both preceding score and paired contrast positive, at least 2/3 informative years positive for contrast, and every leave-one-year-out contrast positive. This is not a statistical significance threshold, a calibrated joint time-series null, or independent geographic confirmation. Historical reference is disjoint in resource years, but the source study has already been published. Do not claim causal selection, true occupancy or evidence of food remaining after winter; animal use/availability labels are source-conditional and spatial choices partly define availability.
