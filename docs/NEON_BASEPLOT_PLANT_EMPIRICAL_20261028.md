## Actual completed source reconstruction

1. The same original checksum-verified NEON RELEASE-2026 mammal data reconstructed the frozen 58 adjacent-season comparisons, 30 mammal trapping grids and 14 physical sites. No observed genus×station overlap or original response Z was recomputed.
2. Official original DP1.10058.001 RELEASE-2026 one-square-metre plant presence and percent-cover source was obtained for exactly these 14 mammal sites, sampling period 2015-04 through 2025-12. **326 source CSV files**, published original bytes **131,730,791**, downloaded with original provider MD5 and byte-size verification. The source contains **355,486 records with plant survey plotID and endDate**. Exact original site+plotID matches the mammal grid ID in all **30 of 30** mammal grids; there were **27,854** matching source rows, of which **27,235** have a numeric original plant or ground-cover observation without the sampling-impractical status.
3. The fixed conservative source comparison uses each original 3-day mammal trapping season A and B with the independent linked base-plot vegetation survey event endDate. A pair is source-qualified only when it has TWO DISTINCT field plant events, one temporally linked to each capture season (not one shared vegetation observation counted twice). At +/-365 days: **51 of the original 58 mammal season pairs** qualify with numeric observed cover records, covering **14 physical sites and 28 original mammal grid plotID values**. The same check at +/-90 days supports **16 of 58 pairs, seven physical sites and 11 original plots**. Thus the previously frozen descriptive threshold of at least 20 pairs/eight sites/12 plots is passed under 365 days but **fails under 90 days**. No temporal gate was relaxed following observation.
4. The source 365-day event-presence-only matching rate was **52/58** pairs; numerical-cover value presence and distinct events reduce this to 51/58. At 90 days, both event-level and numeric-cover distinct comparisons support 16/58.
5. The old empirical remote-sensing source has **36 of 58 original seasonal comparisons across 12 physical sites and 21 mammal grids** within 12 calendar months, with 24,600 actual original station×date CHM raster pixels from 51 provider-verified AOP tiles. We have not cross-tabulated the two new source-eligibility indices at the exact pair level. A valid set bound from the fixed 58-pair universe gives at least **max(0,36+51-58)=29 and at most 36** original seasonal comparisons with BOTH ±12-month full-station CHM and ±365-day two-visit linked field plant availability. This range is a mathematical overlap bound, NOT an observed exact joint source crosswalk. For the stricter ±90-day field plant subset (16/58), the source overlap with CHM can range from 0 to 16; do not claim a joint strict-season result without a direct matching table.

## Biological conclusion and essential distinction

The field plant surveys are **independently observed** and cover herbs, shrubs and ground cover that 1m canopy-height-model values can miss. But the official NEON sampling design places mammal 90×90m grids typically **150 ± 50 m offset** from distributed base plant plots, even when both share one plotID. Hence the 51-pair landscape-neighborhood vegetation source result does not quantify plant cover at the 100 individual mammal trap stations. The 365-day window also allows strong seasonal and year differences in vegetation state. The stricter 90-day subset has too few geographic sites to support a broad macro environmental association.

No genus-specific trap detection p, no animal use-only response and no true habitat-persistence effect was estimated in either source-only programme. The historical ecological result remains conditional genus-associated **capture-space** recurrence across different detected tag sets: corrected original >=3-genus 14-site mean Z+0.795; separate post-result exploratory exactly-two 30-site mean Z+0.615. A persistent genus-dependent trap capture-propensity template remains an alternative to true biological habitat use, as the previously recorded source-free exact fixed-margin counterexample demonstrates. The original negative and source-STOP gates for 10m patch, density W/B, actual trapStatus, tower temperature, release fate and R-W/B matched linkage remain unmodified.

## Single mainline decision

The required large-scale environmental source data now exist at two *different* grains, but their availability does not resolve the causal distinction. Do not open further post-result regressions on historical positive NEON mammal outcomes to find a favorable CHM or ground-cover p-value. The next genuinely independent biological gate requires: (a) trap-scale understory/microhabitat measurement, (b) independently calibrated genus-by-station capture/detection or a second use-observation channel, and (c) a frozen future unseen site/season response cohort. Keep the single unified manuscript in DRAFT/SUBMISSION HOLD; do not create new A/B or method-only paper branches.
