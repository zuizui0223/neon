# San Jacinto held-out cross-scale programme — final status

Date: 2026-09-29

Final decision: **stop_no_confirmatory_crossscale_result**.

## Primary held-out result

Movement family effect: +0.1128 (95% CI -0.2303 to +0.4559).
Three of four species were positive, but the family CI crossed zero.

Packing family effect: -0.1711 (95% CI -1.2118 to +0.8696).
Only one of four species was positive.

All four species point packing effects were inside the pre-specified ±0.5 equivalence margin, but the family 90% CI (-0.9406 to +0.5985) was not contained inside that margin. Therefore small-effect equivalence was not demonstrated.

Neither propagation nor decoupling satisfied the frozen manuscript gate.

## Robustness

N>=2/sex did not rescue either endpoint.

Using the last rather than first nightly capture changed the movement family estimate from +0.1128 to -0.0091, while packing remained similar (-0.1711 versus -0.1427).

Leave-one-species-out movement family intervals all crossed zero. Packing showed large grid-level sign heterogeneity.

## Why first/last matters

Across 2,787 analyzed individual-nights, first and last capture flags differed on 1,413 nights (50.7%). The median first-to-last within-night displacement was 6.25 m, the 90th percentile was 18.75 m, and the maximum was 44.19 m. Median first-to-last elapsed time was 3.62 h.

This explains why a repeated-night movement endpoint based on one representative nightly location is protocol-sensitive. It does not provide a post-hoc alternative confirmatory endpoint.

## Ruling

No manuscript is authorized under the frozen cross-scale rules. The result is retained as a clean held-out negative/heterogeneous validation with a useful protocol-sensitivity lesson.
