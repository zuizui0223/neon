# Why do mammal lineage-specific trapping positions recur?
## Independent environmental-source feasibility gate, NOT a result-selected habitat regression

**Prepared 2026-10-19 AFTER the NEON positive 14-site genus spatial recurrence and direct-null statistical calibration were opened.** No environmental effect has been fitted. The original ecological recurrence hypothesis, original null p=0.001159668, new direct-null p=0.000049998/0.00144993, and the 58-pair/14-site cohort are **locked and unmodified**.

### What biology is genuinely known

In NEON's 14 physical sites, 58 adjacent-season comparisons show significant **genus-associated fixed trap-position recurrence** after excluding shared observed tagged individuals: 11/14 sites have positive null-standardized mean recurrence, and the pre-registered exact physical-site signflip p=0.001159668. A post-result direct fixed-fixed null-site calibration without assuming site-sign symmetry supports the direction under both independent-unit and strongly dependent within-site marginal couplings. This is a **persistent spatial pattern**, not a demonstrated mechanism.

### Mechanisms that are NOT interchangeable

1. **Persistent habitat structure / resource template:** Station-level ground cover, vegetation canopy, shrub/grass cover, soil moisture, microtopography or cover from predators can remain spatially patterned across seasons; different individuals of the same genus might therefore prefer the same trap neighborhoods. Predicts a cross-season lineage spatial pattern associated with directly measured *station-level* habitat, beyond generic trap richness.
2. **Genus-dependent detection / trapping history:** The same positions may consistently catch particular genera even without true ecological use differences, due to placement, bait access, weather exposure, trap operation and species-specific trapping response. Predicts recurrence in marked captures but weak/absent recurrence when capture probability is calibrated with independent camera/telemetry/trap-operation data.
3. **Interactions / dynamic spatial exclusion:** A more abundant congener could alter where animals are trapped or observed. Predicts cross-genus changes aligned with actual changes in relative abundance, after seasonal habitat, individual identity and detection correction. Current NEON historical F/C study has ZERO conservative matched same-season equal-N/U neighbor-composition pairs, so this cannot yet be assigned to interspecific competition.

### External products to check before any mechanism test

- **NEON DP3.30015.001:** annual AOP LiDAR **canopy height model (CHM)**, 1 m pixels, approximately 1 km square tiles. This is the strongest public *mapped environmental structure* candidate for the existing fixed 10 m NEON mammal trap lattice. The CHM is **not** universal microhabitat: understory/shrubs/grass below sensor detection limits may have zero canopy height, and availability of a site-wide AOP flight does not prove a trapping grid's pixels are covered or correctly georeferenced. Official product source: https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP3.30015.001 ; HTML QSG https://data.neonscience.org/api/v0/documents/quick-start-guides/NEON.QSG.DP3.30015.001v1?fallback=html&inline=true
- **NEON DP1.10098.001:** independent field **vegetation structure**, woody and qualifying nonwoody perennial individuals, from **distributed/tower vegetation plots**, not assumed identical to the small-mammal trapping 90×90 m plots. It may offer site background habitat context but needs verified geographic overlap and sampling-year compatibility. Published RELEASE-2026 product: https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP1.10098.001/RELEASE-2026
- **NEON DP1.10058.001:** field plant presence/percent cover, sampled in independent multi-scale vegetation plots; not automatically 10 m mammal trap station cover. Calendar-year 2026 field sampling was suspended, so it cannot be treated as a complete environmental record for the full 2015–2026 mammal window. Official product: https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP1.10058.001
- **NEON locations API:** plot/trap geospatial coordinates need source/quality verification, including any retired/moved trapping sites. NEON's published DP1.10072.001 issue log (as of 2026) identifies **18 affected geoNEON small mammal location positions** across sites **BLAN, HEAL, KONZ, MOAB, ONAQ, SERC, TEAK**. This affects joins to CHM, but not the original *nominal trap label* A1–J10 recurrence test. Data product / locations endpoint: https://data.neonscience.org/data-products/DP1.10072.001 and https://data.neonscience.org/data-api/endpoints/locations/.
