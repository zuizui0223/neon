# Independent UK capture-sequence replication feasibility v1

Date: 2026-10-08.

External source: Brouard MJ et al. 2015, *PLOS ONE*, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0145006; S1 Data DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0145006.s010.

The source article openly provides SessionID, TrapID, Seq, three species capture flags (AsCaught, MaCaught, MgCaught), previous trap occupant (Prev), check lag (Step), time-of-day and habitat. Previous-occupant effects were already established by these authors. We therefore do not treat re-detection of these effects as a new discovery or as independent confirmation of a causal mechanism.

**Before inspecting the UK outcome data**, first verify schema, check-level grain, uniqueness, and whether each session × trap carries a sequence of observations with adequate replication and empty-trap checks. If yes, a narrowly framed external test may compare whether a directed three-species capture-history network exists after conditioning on trap/check opportunity and whether sign/order are stable within sessions. Do not project the Californian six-species ordering onto species with different body-size ecology. Do not claim external validation of San Jacinto-specific dominance directions.

If the file lacks true check-level grain, or only aggregates 'vole vs mouse' without sufficient individual species information, stop. Publish an honest non-comparable-data gate rather than infer transitions from missingness.
