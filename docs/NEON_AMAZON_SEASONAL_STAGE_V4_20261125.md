# Seasonal amplification and attenuation across seed-fate stages: Amazon V4
25 November 2026. Exploratory published-aggregate analysis, no new raw-data fit or independent validation of a mechanism.
Source: Haugaasen et al. 2010, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266467410000027, Experimental design, Tables 1–2 and Other seed predators. Full text checked at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230679775_Seed_dispersal_of_the_Brazil_nut_tree_Bertholletia_excelsa_by_scatter-hoarding_rodents_in_a_central_Amazonian_forest and https://www.academia.edu/89515724/Seed_dispersal_of_the_Brazil_nut_tree_Bertholletia_excelsa_by_scatter_hoarding_rodents_in_a_central_Amazonian_forest. The ambiguous abstract is resolved by methods: 900 seeds per season, 60 stations around 20 trees in one forest, April and September 2006. Original data not retrieved. No seed-level pseudoreplication or new p-values.

Conditional recorded relocation 5.965% vs 1.230%, ratio 4.85191. Identity 12.66667=2.61066*4.85191. Mean reported primary cache time 38.7 vs 11.3 days. Recorded relocations are not an exhaustive fate category: the other four Table 2 final categories sum to 637 and 244, and the relocation counts overlap them. Do not draw relocation and still-there as successive stages. Persistent original primary caches are not all surviving seeds or recruitment; later fates often unknown, five confirmed still there and one observed germinating, no treatment-equivalent germination endpoint. The source already describes seasonal differences in cache longevity and relocation, so only the common-denominator multiplicative description and sensitivity are new calculations.

## Primary-burial observation sensitivity
Table 1 totals 856/639. Excluded/unlisted 44/261. To avoid assuming why rows were excluded, consider all unlisted seeds and three marker-only/unrecovered categories unresolved as to *initial observed fate* (160/459). This deliberately broad ceiling is not a model for lifetime ever-caching, and non-rodent competitors are included in the ceiling. Initial burial fraction bounds 637–797/900 and 244–703/900. Unrestricted wet-dry bounds −7.33 to +61.44 percentage points, sign not identified. If unknown fractions hypothetically have the same burial proportion in both seasons, gap +10.44 to +43.67 points across common fraction 0–1. If none of wet unresolved seeds were buried, 393/459=85.62% of dry unresolved must be assigned burial to tie. If all dry unresolved seeds buried, 66/160=41.25% wet unresolved must be buried to tie. These are sensitivity thresholds only, not justified assumptions, estimates, or confidence intervals. Authors report monkey acquisition 4.8% wet and 27.5% dry (difference +22.7 points), and dry-season peccary destruction of a station. Do not identify exact omitted seed IDs or subtract rounded shares. Seasonal guild composition and detectability remain competing explanations for aggregate contrasts.

## Resource hypothesis status and next gate
Klinger & Rejmanek 2009 DOI 10.1890/07-2146.1 already connected rodent numerical responses, fruit density and stage-specific functional responses in six Belize grids. Yadok doctoral thesis chapter 5 (https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/bitstream/10092/16000/1/Yadok%2C%20Biplang_Final%20PhD%20Thesis.pdf) estimates fruit abundance independently from 537 trees on 17 transects; artificial-seed removal was faster in wet season but removal rate was not detectably related to fruit availability and there was no resolved seasonal difference in fate. Not independent of the earlier conference report; no raw cohort acquired. No ratio-only model rejection, no independently verified factorial seed x consumer density data this turn. Seasonal context may change both scatterhoarder participation and downstream relocation and retention; these are distinct mechanisms. Main next test: stage-specific contrasts in complete matched source cohorts with independent resource measures, consumer density and actor identity; record first encounter guild, cache retrieval guild and tracked fate through a matched horizon. Compare relative resource-only vs scale/guild additions in held-out landscape/year blocks. Do not claim that unmeasured history or resource ratio causes the observed contrasts.
