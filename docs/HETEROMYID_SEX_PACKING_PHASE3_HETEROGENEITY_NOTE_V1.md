# Phase-3 descriptive heterogeneity note

Date: 2026-09-29

Status: descriptive interpretation of the already frozen Phase-3 result. This note does not change the primary decision or define a new confirmatory test.

Primary decision remains:

**no_replicated_positive_support**

## Portal spatial-unit signs

- *Chaetodipus baileyi*: 8 positive / 9 negative plot means; species effect +0.0368.
- *Chaetodipus penicillatus*: 12 positive / 12 negative plot means; species effect -0.0454.
- *Dipodomys merriami*: 7 positive / 10 negative plot means; species effect -0.0055.
- *Dipodomys ordii*: 4 positive / 5 negative plot means; species effect -0.0218.

Portal therefore does not look like a uniformly weak positive effect hidden by low power. The spatial-unit signs are approximately balanced and the equal-species family mean is near zero.

## NEON site signs

- *Chaetodipus penicillatus*: JORN negative, SRER positive; species effect -0.1404.
- *Dipodomys merriami*: JORN negative, SRER negative; species effect -0.2364.
- *Dipodomys ordii*: CPER positive, JORN positive, MOAB positive; species effect +1.6497.

The positive NEON family mean is therefore species-specific rather than a common heteromyid direction. In the frozen leave-one-species sensitivity, excluding *D. ordii* changes the remaining-family mean to -0.1884.

## Allowed interpretation

The data support heterogeneity in sex-specific one-night population packing among species and spatial contexts.

They do not support a general positive heteromyid male-packing effect.

Any mechanistic interpretation of the strong *D. ordii* NEON pattern remains exploratory unless independently supported. The pre-locked recapture validation is not allowed to retroactively promote this species-specific packing result into the primary claim.
