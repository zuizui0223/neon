## Biological target

The only candidate that presently has enough *future* tagged-session
variation to contemplate a spatial response test is **Peromyscus**.
The full five-genus mode comparison and all seven fixed taxon response
predictions are not yet supported by the new prospective capture structure.

The biological distinction is between (W) within-individual 3-night
captured-coordinate variance and (B) dispersion of those same individual's
observed 3-night centroids, *not* territory area, spatial avoidance, or
true activity centre from uncaught animals.
