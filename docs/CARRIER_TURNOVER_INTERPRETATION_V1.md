# Exploratory result: local redundancy, regional carrier turnover

This analysis uses only the already frozen site-level summaries. It does not reopen NEON response data and cannot change the confirmatory decision.

## Positive ecological pattern

The confirmatory result says that species turnover did **not create** site-level spatial continuity: at every site, at least one individual species already satisfied all prespecified adjacency criteria.

The new exploratory result adds a second scale:

> **The species carrying that redundant continuity differ strongly among sites.**

Across 16 sites there are 48 site × continuity-carrier records but 32 distinct carrier species.

- median carriers per site: 2
- range: 1–8
- 20 of 32 carrier species occur as a carrier at only one site
- no carrier species occurs at more than 3 of 16 sites
- only 18 of 120 site pairs share any carrier species
- pairwise carrier-set Jaccard similarity: median 0, mean 0.0316

Thus continuity is **locally redundant but regionally taxonomically replaceable**.

## Richness decomposition

The earlier post hoc result showed that richer sites have more individually sufficient species (Spearman rho = 0.668), while dominance coverage declines with richness (rho = -0.594).

The new decomposition shows that the **fraction** of eligible species that are individually sufficient does not increase with richness (rho = -0.159). Median fraction = 0.477.

This suggests that richer sites deepen redundancy mainly by adding more candidate species, not by making a larger proportion of species spatially sufficient.

Cross-species-only rescue also does not increase with richness (rho = -0.390).

## Ecological interpretation

The strongest positive story is therefore not "turnover is absent."

Instead:

1. local taxonomic pooling usually adds no new spatial continuity;
2. many sites contain multiple independently sufficient species;
3. the identity of those sufficient species changes strongly among sites;
4. richer communities are less dominated by one spatially widespread species yet contain more alternative sufficient species.

This is a two-scale organization of the same aggregate property:

**within-site redundancy + among-site carrier turnover.**

## Boundary

This is descriptive and post hoc. It does not show that carrier turnover stabilizes communities, that the carrier role is a conserved functional trait, or that cross-site taxonomic turnover is unexpected given the geographic spread of NEON sites.
