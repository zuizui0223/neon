# Frozen Holt annual oak seedling / preceding mouse association, 24 November 2026

## What is established, and what is not

[Original source audit 45376258477](https://github.com/zuizui0223/neon/actions/runs/45376258477) verified 39/39 author annual mouse counts against unique observed IDs; all 11,549 capture rows explicitly AUG. 1,132/1,168 historical 2022 target used rows exactly match the 2025 year/ID/station/literal-date tuple with explicit August; 36 unmatched, so historical 100% date gate remains STOP. Plant data re-download SHA256 identical: all invalid original plot labels were 601 (119 seedfall, 4 seedling); nominal in-range same-label overlap 2,479 plot-years, 119 plots and 21 years. Exact red oak species rows shared at only 37 same-label keys, 67 next-label keys. Seedling archive has 23 excess duplicate full keys across all species. No absent species imputed as observed zero and no claim of full 120 traps. Source aggregates are archived.

**Prior art:** Schnurr et al. 2004, Ecology 85:741–755, explicitly predicts high seed predation and reduced recruitment in the year following mast via elevated Peromyscus numbers. Dri et al. 2025, J Anim Ecol 10.1111/1365-2656.70114 showed the acorn–mouse abundance association in Holt. The lagged mechanism itself is not new. The narrower empirical contribution is to evaluate incremental blocked-year prediction of a long-term actual seedling census in that forest, with measured prior mouse counts and seed resource, not simply redescription of the mechanism. No claim of general novelty without further literature review.

## One pre-outcome historical-source contrast

- Freeze the 21 same-label source-eligible census years 1988–2013 already observed by source-only audit, require >=18 with full covariates. One forest, years are the temporal replication units; not 2,479 independent mammal effects. Source counts no individual germination/establishment.
- Use only in-range nominal S1 1,11,...,591 plots with recorded plot-year keys in BOTH archives in EVERY selected year. Same source-key restriction as previous audit, excludes 601 without declaring it erroneous. Remove entire plots with any duplicate exact (year,LINE,PLOT,FIASP 833,SPEC 5) oak seedling key before reading response magnitudes. Require >=100 fixed plots. Use literal red-oak FIASP 833 and SPEC 5 (provider preview identifies these together), report any alternative SPEC codes; no aggregation of unverified other categories. Annual response is sum of recorded HT1 values per 100 fixed surveyed nominal plots, log1p transformed. An unlisted oak is not claimed as a verified individual zero or a germination failure. If no count row, it contributes no recorded count to the aggregate numerator; explicit records and their completeness remain a limitation. HT1 is standing smallest size class, not known newly germinated seedlings.
- Primary annual seed variable is the **previous annual label t−1** of the 2025 authors' independent archive annual red-oak RO series, not a post-outcome choice between the two S1 lag matches. This is conservatively earlier than July t, even if field YEAR labels are collection-year rather than crop-year; exact autumn crop cohort is not established and this is a lagged annual association, not a verified seed-to-germination transition. No same-year August mouse predictor. Previous August nind verified against raw observed unique IDs; not population density.
- Baseline OLS: log1p recorded seedling count per 100 plots ~ year + log1p prior RO + spring minimum temperature + log1p spring precipitation. Augmented: add log1p prior-August nind and prior RO × nind interaction. Standardize predictors using training data only for temporal prediction; full-data standardized coefficients for descriptive association. Spring t weather is published calendar-season covariate, not manipulation. No alternate model selection. Source/year rank/conditioning failures STOP; no new effects if not estimable.
- Three fixed held-out time blocks 1988–1995, 1996–2004, 2005–2013; compare RMSE on the same eligible years. Full-data directional hypothesis: mouse slope at low standardized seed (−1 SD) negative, interaction positive (attenuation at high seed). Require all: both signs, augmented overall blocked RMSE lower, >=2/3 blocks improved, both signs stable for every leave-one-year-out fit (each restandardized). Descriptive stringent rule, not significance test, independent replication, forward prediction or a calibrated joint time-series null. No row-level output or same-data rescue on non-support.
- Deer browsing, forest structure, prior seedling standing stock, age structure, crop label uncertainty, capture/detection, habitat/geographic co-location, and unobserved resource/survival may confound. A positive interaction is compatible with predator satiation but cannot establish predation, dispersal or causal mediation. Do not infer a true regeneration rate or use a ratio with seed denominator. All NEON/Portal/canopy and original Holt STOP results remain unchanged. One manuscript DRAFT / SUBMISSION HOLD.
