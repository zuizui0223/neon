# 24 November 2026 — measured-resource and ground-cover source inventory (effect-blind)

## Ecological direction

NEON exact capture-station recurrence among disjoint observed tags is positive. Same-genus canopy-affinity recurrence at different positions failed its corrected exploratory test. Close that outcome. Ask separately whether changes in **directly measured food-resource patches** predict redistribution of observed capture locations across different individuals/years, beyond persistent site/station effects. This is not a claim that seed resources cause NEON's recurrence, not a test of memory, not a rescue of the same dataset.

Primary external source: Dri et al. (2022), Oikos, DOI 10.1111/oik.09551; author-linked Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.15029247. A 36-year study of rodent capture–mark–recapture and seed production. Its published anticipatory resource-selection test was not supported; reported survival association is PRIOR ART. Our candidate target is response to measured resources **already available before capture** and station-level resource redistribution, not anticipation of future seed crops. Novelty not yet established. No spatial or temporal resolution is assumed from the paper alone.

Secondary inventory only: Brehm & Mortelliti (2021), Ecological Applications, DOI 10.1002/eap.2443; Figshare DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.14488626. Direct fine-scale structural habitat measures near 600 traps in six grids include low vegetation and woody debris. The original published personality–habitat analyses are prior art. This Penobscot source may overlap prior Merz forest studies and is not an independent geographical replication of those studies. It is not a fallback ecological endpoint if primary resource data are unusable.

## Source gate (only metadata/schema, no effects)

1. Resolve author-linked public Figshare article IDs 15029247 and 14488626, provider version, title and DOI. Unversioned paper links require recording the currently public version; this does not assert the raw archive is original to every analysis year. Record provider JSON SHA and exact file IDs, size and computed/supplied MD5.
2. For every declared candidate data file (CSV/TSV/XLS/XLSX/RDS/RData/ZIP/parquet/TXT), bounded whole-file retrieval with public provider link and original MD5 + exact size verification. Budget 100 MB per article, no large or partial selective subset; archive member names or tabular schema only. No processing of biological rows, no animal identifiers, station positions, seed values, effects or plots exported. Unknown formats retain their verified bytes status, not invented schemas.
3. Before opening ecological effects, an additional separate design lock must establish original individual tag, species, capture date and literal station/grid identity, resource measurement date and units and same physical patch mapping, repeated prior resource values and effort/availability. A site-level annual seed index alone does not identify redistribution of resources within a grid. For the secondary archive require measured ground-cover covariate at capture station and dates, not posthoc individuals-only habitat means.
4. Proposed inferential gate (not yet implemented): sufficient independent year transitions and site/grid units, common observation opportunities, disjoint observed IDs, predictor strictly preceding response, conditioning on stable station preference and abundance, compare a source-appropriate held-out year model against a no-resource model. Specify support thresholds and estimand from actual source schema before ecological row-level results. No replacement or relaxing thresholds after effect inspection; archive any source STOP.

Source inventory is not a biological effect or a new paper. The original NEON canopy, density, 10m, Portal temporal fragility, and detection limitations remain unchanged. Only one integrated manuscript, DRAFT / SUBMISSION HOLD.
