## Biological interpretation and cross-source boundary
NY V5/V6 (Vavra & Guiden 2025 DOI 10.1002/ecs2.70178): Dec–Jan maple recorded handling 35→6, carriage 4→5, composition contribution +15.1401–15.4330 seeds, within-taxon −0.6925 to −0.3996, intensity −13.7405. Sparse, one landscape, no equivalence. Maine Aug–Oct pine: positive composition contribution also occurs, but within-taxon contribution is more negative and absolute recorded carriage decreases faster than handling. Thus these two noncommensurate contexts show a common *arithmetic opposing component*, not universal seasonal buffering. Distinct seed types, seasonal intervals, protocols and source units, no pooled effect. Positive composition contribution is not the same as a higher proportion of a fixed disperser guild or a causal effect; interaction weights are not population abundance. Original Merz study already assessed species/personality/availability effects, our new contrast is decomposition of temporal change conditional on offered density and grid. Do not claim plant recruitment or even later cache fate. A future independent prediction should distinguish when changes in actor weights compensate vs fail to compensate within-taxon allocation and activity shifts, with matched seed species, months, effort and full accounting.
