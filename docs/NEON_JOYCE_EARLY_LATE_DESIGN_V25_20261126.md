# V25 — matched-quadrat early retention versus one-year establishment
Joyce et al. 2024 Biotropica DOI 10.1111/btp.13381, public Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.05qfttfb9. Source methods and public README confirm early (7–11 weeks) and late (12–15 months) monitoring of seed addition quadrats, 3024 vs 2880 offered seeds, 368 vs 352 table rows. Sixteen lost quadrats all at site HB (land-use change), no missing-final imputation. Four tree species added in different months July 2021, Apr/Aug 2022; species, exposure duration and calendar not independently varied. Separate tagging study at two sites is not the same seeds and does not identify all early missing as predation. PDF text checked; screenshots attempted and failed. Original authors already report reduced establishment with vertebrate access, and attenuation of the exclusion effect. This is a distinct incremental prediction, not discovery of those effects.

Frozen before original CSV outcome access: download public advertised links and require provider size/checksum; no authentication workaround or guessed file URLs. Exact common key site × species × forest type × station × cage treatment; 368/352 unique rows and exact offered-count totals; late subset with 16 HB rows missing, identical early/late denominators, all four stations and paired arms in each site-species-habitat group, all groups retained. Compute D_early = fraction remaining caged minus exposed; D_late = fraction established caged minus exposed, within matched site-species-habitat groups. Predict D_late in each held-out physical site, including all its forest types, from other sites only. Baseline train species×forest mean late deficit; augmented adds within-stratum centered early deficit times a pooled training slope (OLS, no tuning), clipped [-1,1]. Also compare unchanged transmission D_late=D_early. Equal site-weighted RMSE; minimum six sites and at least two distinct training sites in every tested stratum; descriptive GO only if augmented beats both baselines and >=75% sites improve over stratum baseline. No bootstrap or significance claim, one landscape, no independent time or season validation. This is early retention deficit (not confirmed irreversible predation); no seed-level survival transition, nor separation of vertebrates or cage effects. Camera table inventory only, no resource/consumer adjusted claim. Preserve V24 and all earlier STOPs. No merge/submission.
