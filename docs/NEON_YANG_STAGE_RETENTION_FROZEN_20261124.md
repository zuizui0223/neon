Original analysis already compares scatter hoarding and next-spring survival, and describes seasonal/yearly synchrony. Do not claim the published mechanism as novel. The narrower new descriptive target is within-design **stability of direction of recorded neighborhood contrasts** across life-history stages and across years, not another test of pooled main effects.
