## Important aggregation boundary
V8 pooled actor composition +.11261 to +.12349, but within high density sign unresolved; within low density negative throughout. Equal-density average of the density-specific composition terms has conservative marginal envelope [-0.04956872309067133,-0.018405333213390655] (not exact paired scenario extrema). Since positive pooled composition does not survive this stratification, do not promote a common positive composition mechanism from two sites as a general result. The decomposition is nonlinear: even equal effort weights do not fix the share of handled seeds contributed by each density context and actor. Pooled and stratum-specific terms are different estimands, not inconsistent estimates, a causal effect or proof of a Simpson mechanism. V8 remains valid as the pooled descriptive contrast; V9 narrows its ecological interpretation. The within-taxon component also changes sign across densities; it is not repeated-individual behavior.

## Descriptive compensation threshold and revised plan
For I and B negative, define C/(-I-B) from absolute decomposition dY=I+C+B. NY V5/V6 exact paired endpoint range 1.06929–1.07072; pooled Maine V8 conservative marginal envelope .14877–.17019. The threshold of 1 is arithmetic, not ecological evidence, and the comparisons concern different systems and periods. V9 demonstrates aggregation dependence, so no universal positive C. Main biological question shifts to whether independently measured actor composition/availability and baseline handling traits predict which context shows compensation, under fixed weighting. Avoid further subgroup fishing in consumed V8/V9 data. Next use independent effort-standardized capture records (if actual capture dates/effort and grid-month linkage support the estimand) to test prediction of interaction shares with baseline actor-specific propensities, rather than explain observed outcomes by their own decomposition. A future separate dataset is needed for held-out generality, and true seed survival/establishment needs directly linked follow-up. No merge, submission, or formal meta-analysis.
