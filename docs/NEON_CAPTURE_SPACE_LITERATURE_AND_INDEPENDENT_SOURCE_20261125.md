## Focused prior art review

This is a targeted search, not a systematic review or proof of absence of competing studies. Search terms combined rodent/small mammal, fixed trap station, seasonal spatial segregation, individual identity, turnover, mark-recapture, microhabitat, and public data. Relevant sources checked:

- Chock, Grether and Shier (2022), Oecologia, doi:10.1007/s00442-021-05104-5, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8858926/. Six granivorous rodent species, individual marks, spatial co-occurrence null models at stations on eight grids across four seasons, plus microhabitat resource selection and nightly activity. Prior work already directly addresses fine-scale spatial and temporal niche partitioning. NEON's narrower question is the recurrence of taxon-associated capture positions across seasons after excluding shared observed tags and conditioning on margins. The archive's own San Jacinto species-level 10-pair/6-grid comparator was inspected before the NEON main test; not a new confirmation, not pooled. This comparison is not sufficient to claim the exclusion-of-shared-tags idea is unprecedented.
- O'Fallon, Pinter-Wollman and Mabry (2025), Oecologia, doi:10.1007/s00442-025-05731-2, 2,420 Peromyscus, 36 NEON sites, ten years; individual home-range responses to density, habitat, latitude, sex and condition. Existing continental NEON behavioural analysis; do not claim a first broad-scale study of rodent spatial niches.
- Monitoring small mammal abundance using NEON data: are calibrated indices useful?, Journal of Mammalogy 104:292, https://academic.oup.com/jmammal/article/104/2/292/6845828. Prior detection/abundance calibration; captured tag counts are not true density. 
- Pruvot et al. (2024), One Earth 7:123–135, doi:10.1016/j.oneear.2023.11.003. Study of forest-disturbance gradients, seasonal density and community changes, stratified SCR, not our fixed-station recurrence statistic. Methods document individually marked rodents, per-trap malfunction records and measured station vegetation. Original paper is prior art for environmental and seasonal differences; no novelty claim for these topics.

## Independent candidate: Cambodia

Paper reports nine physical sites, three habitat zones per site, rain and dry sampling, 54 site-zone-season strata; 50 numbered traps at 20-m spacing, eight consecutive nights, 20,214 actual trap-nights, 1,817 captures of 965 reported individuals, 14 small-mammal taxa (13 rodent species and one treeshrew). Five sites sampled 2015–2016, four in 2016–2017. Vegetation survey at each trap location, and malfunctions recorded. Original study estimates densities with stratified SCR. Source DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10042694, explicitly cited by authors. Paper methods retrieved as text from https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/81507/1/81507.pdf; page screenshot requests failed (cache miss), so no claim of visual PDF inspection. Neither the same physical trap locations across both seasons nor the availability of all field tables in the deposit has been confirmed. Published counts are not source data obtained by this project.

Why prioritize: independent tropical system with fixed-number trap grids, taxonomic identity, environmental variation and recorded trap malfunctions. If verified station coordinates/identity, individual mark histories, effort/malfunction and vegetation tables permit joining both seasons, test the analogous label recurrence at fixed stations (without claiming identical 20-m versus 10-m scale) and a separate habitat transfer test on nonidentical stations only if adequate support. No independent detector is documented: cannot establish latent use independently of traps. Use nine physical sites, not 27 zones as independent regional replicates. Preserve rain-to-dry chronology, actual operability, and land-use-zone effects; no selection by outcome. The original paper's study of forest conversion uses spatial contrasts, not measured within-grid conversion through time. Source inventory only this turn: exact author-linked Zenodo record, provider file sizes and MD5, bounded downloads, table schema or archive members. Never execute deposited code or infer undocumented identities. If access fails, report and close this source attempt rather than retrying arbitrary mirrors. No infection analysis, no raw capture locations exported.
