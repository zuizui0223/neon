# MOSA worst-case identity-warning exclusion: effect-blind structural stress test

**Frozen:** 16 October 2026, after reviewing the 14/153-month conflict scope, before looking at any MOSA W/B or MNA-by-space outcome.

## Scientific purpose

This is a *pre-effect sensitivity on observation support*, not an alternate main cohort or a biological-effect analysis. Ask whether the **known** source-identity warning labels could eliminate eligibility for the fixed 153 monthly bouts if every such code were discarded for all dates. This cannot demonstrate correctness of the remaining animals or identify any ecological mechanism.

## Immutable sources and cohort

1. Reverify Zenodo original source checksum, published author RMarkdown's 55 original-date deletions and one 2004 correction; match all 1,089 retained days exactly.
2. Apply first-retained-MN taxon labels as done by the authors; the 14 whitespace-normalization collisions remain excluded, *unchanged*.
3. Use exactly the **153** originally frozen eligible same-calendar-month bouts with three consecutive MN capture-bearing days and at least **five** noncolliding repeat-supported codes (seen on at least two distinct days at valid 30 × 10 trap stations). Never substitute the 363 authors' alternative month-crossing occasions or change the support threshold.
4. Reuse only the already-verified, predictor-only original MNA per month for checking whether at least two distinct levels occur in a year. It must **never** be joined to individual space-use W or B.

## Conservative observation-warning removal

Remove from the *sensitivity support calculation only* each noncolliding code that, anywhere in the author-retained source history, has at least one of:

- more than one distinct valid lattice station on the same capture date;
- two distinct **known** male/female `SX` labels (ignore unknown codes);
- different **known** source species labels (do not reassign taxonomy).

All three classes must be identified before any W/B, centroids or MNA×spatial association is calculated. Avoid treating same-station duplicate rows, unknown sex, or nonblank FLAGS alone as new animals. This is an upper-bound stress test for these observable warning types. Published series prefixes and B suffixes remain literal TOE identifiers. No code is split, rewritten or promoted as correct.

## Frozen output, release rule and scope

Report, **without emitting any individual codes, actual monthly identifiers, capture coordinates, W/B metrics or spatial slopes**:

- original 153 months; original count of repeat-supported code×month units (must be 6,530); independent warning-set counts (38 station, 1 sex, 0 species, union 39) must reproduce;
- number of months and repeat-code/month units touched by warnings (must match previous 14 and 15);
- number of the **original 153** months remaining m>=5 after discarding all 39 warning codes, and number newly below m=5;
- count of years with at least two months having different original MNA values among the retained m>=5 months, and number of retained months within those years;
- m decrease and support by original month **only as aggregate histogram/range**, with no favorable subsets selected;
- decision: `OBSERVATION_SUPPORT_STRESS_PASS` only if at least 60 of the original 153 months and 10 distinct varying-MNA years (and at least 60 months within those years) remain. A PASS is **NOT** permission to open W/B or a claim of biological individual identity. A FAIL is a genuine structural limitation.

- Independent underlying animal identity and the published 19,850 MN-animal count remain unresolved pending authoritative corrections.
- No automatic source-effect release, no post-result recoding, no inference of territoriality or density-accommodation mechanism.
