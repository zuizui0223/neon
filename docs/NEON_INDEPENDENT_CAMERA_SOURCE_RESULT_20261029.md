# NEON 29 October 2026: genuinely independent camera records, but no same-time trap station replication

## Completed source-only result

The independent 2026-published camera detection data at Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20826511 were retrieved **as real original external source data**, not inferred from a citation. Original public byte-level MD5 was verified for all three original files, comprising 3,479,745 source bytes. The camera dataset covers 820 valid deployments and 31,775 reported image-sequence rows from projects across 2015–2022.

A strict classification distinguishes target rodent genus-level camera detections from larger Sciuridae and unclassified Small Mammal/Animal labels. There are 47 time-valid original target small-rodent genus detections (Cricetidae/Heteromyidae/Dipodidae/Muridae) among six camera deployments and two genera. Another 2,396 source genus-level rodent camera detections are from different rodent families; this is not evidence that these are the original NEON mammal target taxa. The camera catalogue has 2,605 sequence rows whose deployment ID is not in the verified deployment table and nine other records outside the valid registered camera deployment interval; these are source metadata limitations and were not assumed to represent animal absence.

The entire original checksum-verified NEON RELEASE-2026 mammal 58 adjacent-season comparison / 30 trapping grid / 14 physical site universe was separately reconstructed, with 339 distinct plot×capture-day opportunities. We then queried the original date-valid official NEON E5 location histories for all 30 original mammal grids and compared public camera deployment coordinates/dates against the same original source. At the **spatial** level, only five camera-to-grid-center pairs fell within 250 m of a verified original E5 position (the same 5 original grids); 57 camera/E5 plot links were within 1 km and 271 within 10 km, but neither is a station-level match.

**Crucial negative source-eligibility fact:** ZERO of the five camera-to-NEON-E5 links within 250 m had a camera operating on any of the original mammal trapping days. Hence there are ZERO original season-pair overlaps suitable for a same-station, same-date genus camera-vs-Sherman trap comparison. Since no pair passed the predeclared conservative spatial+temporal **prefilter**, the subsequent 100 actual trap-coordinate source check was not opened or imputed from E5. This is not a claim that camera and trapping datasets never overlap at broad ecological sites; it means no original 58-comparison NEON same-time station cross-detection support was established by this independent dataset.

The source-only GO/STOP definition required 20 original pairs across eight independent physical sites and twelve grids, with real camera source within 20 m of a source-verified original trap and at least two independently camera-identified target small-rodent genera during both original mammal seasons. **Actual result SOURCE STOP.** The original NEON positive observed genus capture-space Z, historic 10m patch non-support and all failed density, tower-weather and trap-operability guards were not refitted.

## Biological significance, neither overstate nor discard

The independent camera catalogue confirms that a separate recording TECHNOLOGY is available in parts of the ecological geography, and rare genus-level small rodent records exist. It does **not** cross-validate the original season-to-season NEON capture-station recurrence due to mismatched dates and low genus-level coverage at matched stations. The camera source is overwhelmingly a single short (~1 month) deployment per original subproject, not a longitudinal paired-season small-mammal camera array. Unknown camera labels like Small Mammal are not silently recoded as Peromyscus or Microtus. Even paired cameras and live-traps can have correlated detection biases due to habitat, camera triggering, placement, light, bait and animal behavior; distinct instruments do not guarantee conditional independence.

A 2024 independent paired rodent camera/live-trap study (Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution, DOI 10.3389/fevo.2024.1359201) reported Microtus californicus on cameras but zero live captures, demonstrating in one external system how detector choice can differ in the set of observed rodents. That publication is a **literature example**, not new data obtained and analyzed by this NEON project and not a source for the original 14-site ecological Z.

This result closes this particular 2026 Zenodo independent-detector opportunity for original NEON season-pair validation; it does NOT falsify habitat selection or mammal spatial niche memory. A credible next biological test needs a genuinely prospective or independently sampled multi-season rodent camera/live-trapping dataset with direct trap-level vegetation and documented per-detector functioning. Existing environmental source GO results remain: 30 original pairs at 12 sites/19 grids with source historical CHM and separate nearby plant plot survey visits (365-day window), but both are environmental measurements, not a second animal observation channel. Moreover 53/58 original seasonal comparisons used the same nearest AOP month for both seasons, precluding independent two-time canopy-change inference.

Keep the ONE unified mammal spatial macroecology manuscript v0.9 in DRAFT/SUBMISSION HOLD. Its strongest verified ecological observation is repeated genus-associated exact trap capture locations despite nonshared observed tagged mammals; no claimed causal persistent niche, habitat mediation, demographic replacement or competition.
