# Direct seed-fate stages: independent source gate, 24 November 2026

## Why this is a new source direction, not a rescue

Holt recorded smallest-class seedling associations retained resource-dependent directions, but the stricter past-trained prediction failed. Close that endpoint. To distinguish the ecological functions of mammals, use experimental studies of seed fate rather than more models of standing seedling counts. The existing NEON conditional same-station recurrence is distinct; these experiments cannot establish its causal mechanism.

Primary source: Xiao & Zhang 2016, Journal of Animal Ecology, DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.12542, deposited Figshare 3189775 v1. This is a replacement experiment of palatable chestnuts and less palatable acorns (mixed vs single-species patches, late October–December 2008). The original paper already reports that removal/caching and embryo-intact dispersal differ, and already describes apparent mutualism/competition depending on fate. Published embryo removal among cached acorns 52% mixed versus 8% single-species (rounded). Hence, **conditional on caching**, the ratio of intact fractions is 0.48/0.92 ≈ 0.522, and the cumulative cache probability would have to increase >0.92/0.48 ≈ 1.92-fold to increase the probability of an embryo-intact cache per released seed. This is illustrative algebra of published conditional percentages, not a fitted new result, significance test, actual recruitment, or exact raw-data ratio. Source: https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1365-2656.12542.

Secondary complementary source: Kuprewicz 2015, PLOS ONE 10.1371/journal.pone.0124932, Figshare 1256359 (resolve public version). Experimental caches vs surface seeds and plant life stages in a tropical forest. Published benefits and disadvantages at different stages are prior art. Distinct manipulation, not a replicate of mixed-seed patches. Neither paper is a new proof of seasonal switching. Li et al. 2021 already reported spring/autumn differences (10.3897/zookeys.1025.60972); not adding figures as new data. Other candidates include Guo et al. 2026 Dryad 10.5061/dryad.g79cnp64s (artificial seeds, relative frequency, two November seasons, no germination), Cao et al. 2018 Dryad 10.5061/dryad.4vh37hn (cache pilferage and seed fate), and Sachser et al. 2025 original beech fate study already audited. No Dryad access workaround or outcome pooling in this run.

## Source-only gate and next estimand

Fetch exact public source metadata and bounded original files via Figshare API; verify article/version identity, provider sizes/MD5, SHA256; inspect archive members and tabular schemas only. Primary source identity is exact v1. Secondary use declared current public version with title corroboration and explicit version record; do not assume archived author dataset has a particular version. Do not use article effect estimates in source selection. No rows of biological response values exported or analyzed. Unknown format or provider access failure is STOP, not zero. No secondary substitution if primary endpoint fails.

Before effect fitting: establish experimental release denominators, paired station/treatment or randomized blocks, seed species, date or duration, removal and caching categories, embryo integrity, missing fate, and whether these columns refer to the same released cohort. Stage-conditional denominators and their overlap must be verified. For primary raw data, the planned incremental question is the **attenuation of a neighborhood effect from primary caching to intact caching**, with a precommitted paired-block contrast on probabilities per released seed, explicit unknown-fate bounds, and no identification of a true plant benefit beyond measured follow-up. Define exact numeric gates after source schema, before effect values. For secondary source, establish whether germination and later seedling survival are followed for the same individual seed or separate experiments; no multiplying unlinked marginal outcomes. These stages can inform a future comparative synthesis, but they cannot be pooled as one effect with the Holt annual regression, which remains closed. A genuine seasonal test needs comparable deployment and endpoint windows and at least two seasons, which are not yet established in either candidate. No novelty claim until independent sources and prior literature checked.
