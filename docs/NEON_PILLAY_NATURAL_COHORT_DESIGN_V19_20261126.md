# V19: natural cohort and vertebrate-exclosure source gate
After V18 conditional projection and Guo HTTP403, no further Dryad workaround or new capture-only models. Existing Holt red-oak endpoint is standing HT1 stock, not recruitment, and its past-trained stock-adjusted forecast failed; original STOP preserved. Gratzer et al. 2022 DOI 10.1111/oik.08826 already described attenuation of seed-rain variation with later mortality; Dryad j3tx95xfz file listing seed rain and trap coordinates only, no joint seedling histories. Wright et al. 2005 DOI 10.1890/03-0750 already reported seedling density generally increasing despite declining per-seed recruitment with seedfall density; generic rate-vs-count non-proportionality is prior art.
 
One bounded original source: Pillay et al. 2018 DOI 10.1002/ece3.4352, public CC BY4.0 Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.2020340, MD5 4f5b6f4c58b43f488f43aab675562d9c. 2014 single mast event, Sabah; wind-dispersed Dryobalanops lanceolata, seed predators include pigs and rodents. Source metadata documents 13 trees, 312 seed traps (24 per tree), 2069 naturally arriving individual seed records, 1540 added-seed records in 56 paired 1 m² exclusion/control plots. Densities 5 seeds/m² at 2 m, 50 at 32 m; density is confounded with distance by design. No direct seasonal or mammal-specific movement contrast. Exclosure randomized paired placement, does not isolate individual animal taxa and may have cage effects. 
 
Before outcomes: verify file checksums, source headers and expected row counts, plot and seed identity, dates and censoring. Lock primary new estimand: in seed-addition plots, observed 30-day survival fraction and number per 1 m² plot, within each forest × density-distance treatment. SURV=1 death by day 30 => not alive, duration >30 => alive at 30, censored before 30 => unknown; validate date and AGE consistency and literal coding before rates. Use confirmed count plus missing/censored bounds, no false zeros or all-death imputation. Compare exclosure versus open treatment only within a fixed density-distance context and forest; preserve tree-level clustering and unequal replicates. Require full 5/50 seed counts per plot, matched open/exclosure plot counts per tree × distance, no reassigning identities. Density-distance contrast (not density causal) compares observed absolute and proportional survival in both treatment contexts. Exact same analysis dates, no selection based on observed direction. Natural seed fate records are a separate observational cohort; do not use seed traps as denominator of unlinked seed-level histories, infer zeros in omitted plots, or infer actual natural dispersal. No prediction of emergence timing from final stage alone. No new outcome computed during source audit. No merge/submission.
