# Real independent camera-trap detector as a possible NEON mammal niche discriminator

29 October 2026. One NEON mammal ecological macro paper, PR #23, DRAFT/SUBMISSION HOLD.

## Why a new independent biological observation channel is now the central test

The original positive NEON fixed-margin genus-to-trap seasonal capture-space pattern (14 independent sites/58 original comparisons, observed Z approximately 0.795) and exploratory exact-two genus 30-site pattern Z approximately 0.615 cannot by themselves distinguish genuine recurring space use from a persistent genus-dependent catchability/observation station template. Even comprehensive ground vegetation or AOP canopy does not independently calibrate an animal observation process. DNA sequences, fecal records and rodent pathogen specimens come from the same original Sherman live-trapping channel.

## Newly discovered data source, dated before this source test

Public Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20826511, published June 24, 2026: Standardized camera trapping data from National Ecological Observatory Network subprojects and other forest plots. Three published source files: camera_trap_deployments.csv, camera_trap_sequences.csv, subproject_descriptions.csv, with provider MD5 digests embedded in the source-only runner. Includes 13 NEON-associated forested subprojects and four additional US forest field sites, observations over 2015–2022. Each camera subproject is surveyed only approximately one month, so the dataset does NOT guarantee capture-season repetition. It has independent camera photographs of at least some Peromyscus and other rodent taxa, but source images and vague Small Mammal labels do not constitute validated genus identities.

## Pre-defined prospective source QA, no original mammal effect opened

1. Require the full exact original 58 seasonal comparisons, 30 NEON mammal 100-trap grids, 14 independent physical sites, original six actual capture days and original source date-effective official NEON station histories. Reconstruct only from checksum-verified NEON RELEASE-2026. Keep all four original NEON published location-issue grids in the source population.
2. Fetch the three public original Zenodo CSV files (source DOI above) and verify each byte stream against its original public MD5 digest and a combined 9 MB source budget. Parse all deployments (name, coordinate, actual start/end), sequences (date, class/order/family/genus) and subproject descriptions. This external dataset is NOT NEON's same live-trap DNA/pathogen product.
3. Use the original official NEON named station E5 source REST history, at all actual source mammal trapping dates, to transform original source UTM positions. Compare each original external camera coordinate with the original 30 E5 points. First screen <=250m proximity to the original mammal grid's central E5 and at least one matching original capture day within the camera deployment interval. Also record descriptive 1km, 5km and 10km proximity without claiming station scale. Co-location of E5 alone never proves co-location of another camera and a Sherman live trap.
4. For every date-and-E5 candidate original grid, fetch the actual NEON source coordinates of ALL 100 literal A1–J10 stations, with their original capture-date histories. No assumed grid rotation or synthesized trap coordinate. Only a camera within 20m of one original actually dated station can be considered a candidate station-near independent observation. The camera must have actual rodent genus detections on original capture dates, not merely a camera deployed elsewhere at the same NEON ecological site.
5. Define taxonomically identifiable nocturnal small-rodent target for independent camera evidence as verified Mammalia/Rodentia with a known genus in Cricetidae, Heteromyidae, Dipodidae or Muridae. Report Sciuridae separately, and NEVER convert camera records classified only as Small Mammal or Animal into Peromyscus, Microtus or another positive genus. Camera absence cannot be assumed true animal absence, and source deployment duration does not certify every camera performed perfectly.
6. Source-level NEXT BIOLOGICAL GO would require >=20 original season pairs at >=8 independent physical sites and >=12 original grids with actual cameras within 20m of source-verified live trap station(s) AND at least two camera-identified small rodent genera re-detected independently in both original A and B trapping seasons. Report any direct genus camera observations honestly even when this gate STOPs. No redefinition of E5 250m as trap co-location, no rescue from later camera records or vague guilds.
7. Publish only aggregate counts, public DOI, published source checksums and source outcome category; no NEON original site/plot identifiers, station coordinates, original animal IDs, source capture dates, raw camera coordinates or individual detection records. Do NOT compute any original mammal genus-at-trap Z, test its correlation with camera source, infer true animal use, infer camera's perfect detection or estimate competition.

This external source can establish whether a genuinely different observation technology exists at the required NEON spatial+temporal grain. Even camera data that happen to overlap original Sherman traps may exhibit detection covariance and species-specific trigger errors; real detection-corrected niche estimates require a prospectively identifiable multi-method observation model and a held-out cohort, not another favorable old-release p-value.
