# Do spatial niche templates reassemble after individual turnover?
## Independent NEON continent-scale validation of the San Jacinto ecological signal

**Frozen 2026-10-19 before opening any cross-season spatial recurrence in NEON.**
**Scientific category:** a *separate* independent ecological generalization hypothesis motivated by the positive San Jacinto cross-season test. This is **NOT a rescue** of the original 8-genus density packing failure or the later unconfirmed conspecific-versus-congeneric crowding contrast. The NEON cross-season recurrence endpoint has not been opened in this branch.

### Biologically falsifiable question

Do local small-mammal **genera** recover their characteristic fixed trap positions in another season even when the individual animals occupying those positions are entirely different, more than expected solely from each season's genus trap occupancy and local trap richness?

San Jacinto (one study area, 6 physical grids, 10 eligible units) previously showed a positive cross-season species-specific trap recurrence beyond season-B fixed-fixed margins after removing the same individuals from both seasons: 9/10 positive grid-transition Z, 5/6 positive physical grid means, exact grid sign-flip p=0.046875. A positive *multi-site NEON genus-level* result would extend the ecological principle to geographically independent habitats and lineages, but is not an exact taxon-rank or field-protocol replication of the source species-level test. The San Jacinto result was explicitly visible **before** freezing this new NEON test.

### Population and observation design fixed before NEON outcomes

- Original **RELEASE-2026** small mammal live-trap source DP1.10072.001, 2015-04 through 2026-09. Use only the versioned checksum/byte-verified **basic** `mam_perplotnight` and `mam_pertrapnight` source via the existing authorized manifest downloader. No later provisional observations, no new W/B or density response.
- Primary physical unit is `site × plotID`, nested in **site** (independent geographic unit for inference). A source session is exactly 3 distinct trap nights with canonical `gridCompletion='setting complete, processing complete'`, valid capture dates within four consecutive calendar dates, and a standard 10×10 A1–J10 nominal trap-lattice. Exclude known special sampling site SRER from the *primary* canonical 10×10 comparison; report it as an excluded site, not as a favorable subgroup.
- Four meteorological seasons: winter (December–February, December assigned to the following season-year), spring (March–May), summer (June–August), autumn (September–November). One bout per `site × plot × season-year × season`: earliest *completed*, protocol-compliant 3-night event by first date (ties by eventID), selected before opening any genus trap positions. Do not pool multiple bouts into a single artificially high-occupancy season.
- Consecutive seasonal ordinal pairs only, e.g. 2023 spring→summer, 2023 fall→2024 winter. If one season is missing, **do not bridge** it; no arbitrary time-window expansion to acquire favorable results.
- Taxa: stable **genus** inferred from official source `target` taxonID lookup; species-group and uncertain Peromyscus species remain **Peromyscus** rather than being assigned to one species. Any `site×plot×tagID` that has multiple conflicting genera in complete-source capture events is omitted throughout the source study (report its count). Missing tagID, target genus or valid trapCoordinate is not used as a known animal. One tag within a site×plot is one observational identity assumption, not independent biological truth.
- For a given seasonal pair, remove from **both seasons** every tag found in both sessions (regardless of its genus); calculate numerator/occupancy only from season-exclusive captures. This removes the trivial same-animal site-fidelity explanation; unresolved tag reuse remains an observation limitation.
- Capture effort: require three complete, source-compliant nights at each session; require >=90 distinct trap station labels observed on **every** night (effort record, not capture count), and report when this fails. Collapse repeat capture records per animal×night×station to one; if a tag on one night is at multiple distinct stations, exclude its records in that bout as ambiguous; report frequency.

### Prespecified structural support gates

A genus qualifies within a seasonal pair if it has >=2 **season-exclusive, consistently genus-labelled marked animals** in *each* season, and >=2 distinct occupied valid trap positions in each season. An eligible pair requires **>=3** such shared genera, source protocol compliance as above, and at least **8 distinct physical NEON sites with >=1 supported seasonal pair and at least 20 eligible site×plot×season transitions** across all sources. If either replication floor fails, **STOP** without opening the recurrence outcome. The complete support receipt must report number of eligible sites, plots, pairs, years, genera, excluded shared animals, and proportion of potential season pairs failing each gate. Every physical site is reported only as a count; no animal tags or sensitive source identifiers in the receipt.

A diagnostic only (never to rescue primary failure) may count >=2 shared genera with same 2-animal and 2-station requirements. Diagnostic results do not authorize an ecological effect when the >=3-genus primary gate fails.

### Spatial ecology endpoint (only AFTER source support gate passes)

For each eligible pair u, construct genus×trap binary occupancy from **all valid, season-exclusive, target individual captures** of *eligible shared genera*, separately for season A and season B on the nominal 100-position lattice. Each individual may occupy multiple observed stations; occupancy is 0/1 per genus×station.

- Fixed season-A matrix `A`; season-B genus×trap `B`.
- Primary observed count `O_u = Σ_{g,t} A[g,t]B[g,t]` (same-genus same-trap recurrence across seasons by **different tagged animals**).
- Frozen null: randomize only B using **fixed-fixed 2×2 swap/curveball** operations preserving *each genus's number of occupied traps* AND *each trap's season-B genus richness*. Keep A and the exact occupied season-B trap labels, source sample size, grid edges, and identity removal fixed. Thus significance cannot arise solely from common high-activity traps, genus prevalence or season-B trap richness. Use fixed seed 2026101901, 2,000 independent/approximately independent null samples per unit, 1,000 burn-in swaps and 40 constrained swaps between recorded samples; report acceptance and autocorrelation diagnostics. If swaps never move or null SD=0, report such units as uninformative instead of manufacturing a Z.
- For informative units, `Z_u=(O_u−E_null O_u)/SD_null O_u`. Multiple seasonal pairs within the same site are not independent. Average Z within each **physical site** to form `z_site`, then primary test the **mean of site means** against zero using exact sign-flip enumeration when eligible informative sites <=18, otherwise 20,000 fixed-seed site sign flips. The predeclared *one-sided* alternative is greater than zero. Pass ecological sign criterion only when >=8 informative sites, mean site Z>0 and p_site<0.05. Also report number of positive site means, spatial/geographic balance, and how many units/sites were excluded for zero null variance. There is no separate significant unit-level claim.
- No adjustment for soil, habitat, climate, vegetation, predator density or phylogeny is opened in this first pass, preventing post-hoc explanatory variable fishing. These may be distinct follow-ups only if independent source-backed measurements justify them.

### Ecological inference boundary

Even a positive site-level result would support only: **genus-associated trap-position templates persist across seasonal replacement of tagged individuals beyond fixed genus and trap occupancy margins**, not that the exact same individuals return, that competition causes the pattern, that per-trap microhabitat properties were measured, or that true activity centres/home ranges were obtained. Possible mechanisms include habitat filtering, habitat-linked detectability, stationary traps, demography, congeneric interactions, and seasonal environmental persistence. Trapping protocol and geography differ from San Jacinto and should not be ignored.

A null or STOP result remains a substantive negative/exclusion finding; do not tune 2/genus support, change time-window, switch to species-level labels or favorable sites after seeing results. The previous original 6/8 genus-density generality failure, provisional Peromyscus nonreplication, U-adjusted same-total exact-pair STOP, and MOSA effects seal are all unchanged.
