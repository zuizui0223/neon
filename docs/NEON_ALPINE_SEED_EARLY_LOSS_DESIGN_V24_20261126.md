Two alpine sites, four elevations, 2021–23 seed tracking, crop and rodent monitoring, simulated-cache emergence in 2022/24. Proposed incremental test: can independently measured tracking predation fraction predict the subsequent open-vs-excluded emergence contrast for matched site/elevation/release-year units, beyond actual resource and rodent counts?
