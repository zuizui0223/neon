## Separate prospective exploratory contrast
Spring-only response does not require future autumn observations. This different estimand was fixed after source support but before resource masses or grouped pregnancy effects were opened. It uses all 32 sites and the same original pinned release/file manifest. Compare adjacent years of spring observations (2018–2025, pairs indexed 2019–2025) with preceding autumn changes (2017–2024). No post-result selection of lag, season, or threshold.

LTR User Guide vF (https://data.neonscience.org/documents/10179/2838366/NEON_LTR_userGuide_vF/860ff6ce-9a5d-7630-e1b8-b419e19db437?download=true&version=1.0), sections 3.2, 3.3, 3.5, 3.9: elevated 0.5 m² traps, date intervals, 2018–19 sorting reductions, 2020 trap number reductions, QA reweighing. Seed group includes fruit and attached material. This is measured partial-autumn seed-group deposition in grams per trap-day, not actual edible seed biomass. Only complete recorded intervals wholly within [Sep 1, Dec 1], trappingDays equals date difference, original OK or explicit empty TE with no mass child, stable elevated trap metadata. Unique field parent and original N measurement with one massSampleID; Y is not added. No seed absent -> zero assumption; exclude positive/unresolved Mixed and unresolved original Seeds. TE is explicit empty field status, not missing group imputation. Non-overlap, same physical literal trap and plot metadata in both years; >=45 days of common coverage, <=14 days symmetric difference of covered day sets, no prorating. Require at least three traps in three plots. Average per-trap difference in deposition flux (not whole-site total or full autumn). No normalization by vegetation cover; sample condition flags beyond trap condition remain an acknowledged limitation.

Mammal: original completed three-night event selection via existing _events, earliest spring bout per plot, >=90 canonical trap coordinates each night. Compare same plot IDs across consecutive years with first bout dates within 14 calendar days. Explicit species rank and binomial; tagged adult F only; exclude observed tag species/sex conflicts and replacement-coded tags (no guessed tag crosswalk). One earliest adult female capture per tag within shared selected plots, same-day status conflict unknown, pregnant/not explicit. >=10 known state tags per species and year. All unknown earliest statuses retained in worst-case bounds. Not population birth or pregnancy probability independent of capture. No attempt to infer lactation, age of reproduction, or entire-season onset.

Align changes in recorded pregnancy fraction to direction of prior fall flux change (higher-resource year's rate minus lower-resource year's rate). All qualifying nonzero resource changes, all species comparisons; equal physical site-weighted mean. Report all support and effects even if negative or thin. Exploratory direction-consistency criterion >=8 sites, >=20 species-year-pairs, >=4 sites with two or more year-pairs, positive equal-site mean, >=75% positive sites and positive leave-one-site-out means. Unknown-pregnancy marginal extreme bounds are not sampling CIs. No p-value or independent pair assumption. Site/taxon comparisons may share years, species or populations; dates, weather, density and cohort composition may covary; no causal claim, no climate adjustment, no inference about caching, competition or coexistence. If source stop or thin support, report it without changing thresholds. Full shard success and file counts mandatory. Raw identifiers remain ephemeral.
