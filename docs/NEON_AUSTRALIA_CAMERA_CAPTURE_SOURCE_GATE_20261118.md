# Matched camera-visit versus live-trap-capture source gate — Australia (source-only)

Date: 18 November 2026. Same unified NEON mammal ecology paper, PR #23 **Draft / SUBMISSION HOLD**. This is **a new, independent original field data source**, but the underlying source is an *already-published* observational study; not a pristine confirmatory ecology experiment or new causal detector calibration.

## Purpose and explicit biological limit

Ask whether spatial patterns inferred from mammal **captures** can differ from those of verified **visits to the same stations**, even when species, site, season, and observed trap state can be matched. A difference would challenge treating repeat capture stations as an unbiased animal resource niche. It would NOT establish movement, habitat preference, individual social contact, demographic replacement or actual camera-detection probability, because cameras themselves are aimed at baited Elliott live traps.

Published prior art: Gil-Fernández, Le Roux & Carthey, *I'm not falling for that: Boldness and trappability of small mammals decline in urban reserves*, Dryad 2026-01-05 DOI **10.5061/dryad.18931zd8w**. The authors already reported lower boldness and trappability in urban reserves, with different dominant *Rattus* taxa. These differences must NOT be promoted as novel outcomes. Design: five urban reserves paired with five reference forest sites in New South Wales, camera traps observing Elliott-trap stations, spring/autumn sampling. Original study text is provenance, not verification that all individual CSV rows are usable.

## Effect-blind structural GO/STOP

Read all original CSV rows before outcome opening. Restrict to exactly published small mammal species (unambiguous original species strings) and no inferred observations for missing camera times. Require:
1. Exactly **two source site types** corresponding to forest and urban reserves, at least **8 actual sites**, at least **30 camera site×station identifiers**, both spring and autumn observed; no new sites added from unrelated literature.
2. Source camera and captures each show >=8 originally named overlapping sites (capture-site coverage need not be a one-to-one camera-effort denominator); >=5 site×season groups with actual camera and trap records; at least 2 nonempty camera species names. Publication's site-level effort file must be present and numeric, without inventing nightly station-specific camera uptime.
3. At least 100 distinct valid `visit_ID` values in the original camera/interaction files, and an explicit camera `trap_state` column with ≥90% nonmissing original records. Whether a visit was captured, whether trap was open, and whether behavior was shy are **not** included as a positive ecological result in this source-only gate.
4. Original live-trap `trap_ID` and camera `station` must have uniquely recoverable **site-specific station keys** by exact equality or an unambiguous source-prefixed exact station identifier. If ambiguous or <20 unique matched site×station keys, stop and do not use ad hoc spatial joins.
5. Site-and-season matching uses only original source keys and stated fields. Capture `date` and camera `Date` must both parse with a deterministic explicit-format parser; no arbitrary ±1-day shifts or guessed session offsets. Require ≥5 site×season with both dated modalities before an outcome test.
6. Blind source record needs a correct source digest/size receipt, first. No raw site×species effect sign, capture rank, or urban-vs-forest conditional outcome may be opened during source-only audit.

If any condition fails, source **STOP**, log which requirement and whether it was an API HTTP 403/HTML, schema mismatch, missing original station crosswalk, or scientific support. Do not change thresholds to salvage a result. Each missed component is reported, not imputed.

## Predeclared possible downstream ecological test, *not* an executed effect

If and only if source GO, construct matched exact site×station×season×taxon×night co-occurrence, with camera visits classified as **independent events**, and observed captures with explicit capture dates. Report two original exposures separately: camera sampling nights per site and original camera-visible `trap_state`, neither of which proves live-trap operability or camera visibility at every hour.

Primary contrast: **does the rank/order of within-site captured taxa by station differ from camera-visit station rankings beyond a species-blind same-station baseline**, and how much is attributable to event-level trap interaction/open state? Block-resample original site pair (urban–forest matching only if original pairing is verifiable), otherwise independent physical site. Do not assume camera non-detections are true absences or normalize all stations to equal trap effort. Prefer genuinely held-out site×season checks over selecting a favorable subgroup. Stop if source does not permit matched modality denominator or if pairing information is missing.

Positive capture/visit disagreement would support observation-process sensitivity in *this baited Australian trap system*, NOT automatic dismissal of NEON genera's 14-site conditional capture-space Z, proof of true habitat niches, or globally general detector bias. Null disagreement would be a reference-conditioned non-support, not proof live traps measure true habitat faithfully.

**No biological direction, metric, null or filtering rule is to be altered after looking at Australian outcome.** The original NEON 14-site Z=+0.795, Portal 436 non-support, Finland 10-site stationary-individual negative relative residual, NEON density 6/8 genus FAIL and Structural mapped island species results remain unchanged and not quantitatively pooled.
