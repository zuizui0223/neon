## Novelty and next gate
Authors already described winter foraging decline, taxon-specific seed preferences and different in-situ consumption. New contribution is recorded whole-carriage/handling decomposition with exact absent-actor bounds, not discovery of taxon differences. This empirical pilot distinguishes total interaction intensity, interaction-weighted composition, and taxon-specific outcomes. In other independent seasonal actor-resolved studies predefine comparable seed/period/site/effort, reconcile source counts, require at least two focal taxa and repeated handling support across sites/periods; preserve sparse or absent actors as bounded. No pooling with primary burial, cache survival, or untracked disappearance. For population composition vs activity use independent density or occupancy data; for individual plasticity use repeated marked individuals. Brehm & Mortelliti (2022) PNAS https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2113870119 reports individually tagged deer mice and directly observed positive/negative seed events, data https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.16455570; different design (one nonmast year), repeated seasonal support not yet verified. No new result from that source this turn.
