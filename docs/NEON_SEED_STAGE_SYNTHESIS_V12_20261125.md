## Manuscript decision
The supported claim is stage-specific and context-dependent observed non-proportionality, not a universal tendency for buffering, positive composition, or proof that movement reduces germination. Algebraic Q identity is not novelty; next independent test should predict Q from independently measured season/resource/consumer covariates, with same focal seed taxon, effort, follow-up, actual nested transitions and independent landscape blocks. Predefine whether outcome is carriage among handled, cache among removed, or post-carriage survival. Do not pool unlike transitions or create a common stage chain across different studies.
