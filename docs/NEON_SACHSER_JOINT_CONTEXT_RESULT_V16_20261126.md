S3 two grids of 50 traps, five August nights each; reported counts may include recaptures and trap operability not independently known. Both sites have the five dates. Relative beech-rain/capture index ratio 6.840 and three-species rain/capture ratio 2.631 are dimensionless changes of two measured proxies; NOT per-capita seed availability.

The source's separate germination batches (4×100 and 8×100) gave lab mean +9.75 pp. In contrast, field total germination change allowing all missing fates: [-28.33, -11.67] pp. Yet field intact-or-germinated change bounds [-12.78, 3.89] pp and predation change bounds [-3.89, 12.78] pp both cross zero. Thus decline in documented germination cannot establish increased mortality or future establishment decline; intact seeds may have delayed germination or later die. The two laboratory cohorts are not randomized field exclusions; no causal distinction between environment, viability selection, animal behavior, and phenology. No sampling CI, no site-level matched resource/fate data, one forest/two viable years; no temporal seasonal generality. No regression, no extrapolation into NEON.

## Decision
Resource context can change in both quantity and taxonomic composition; capture composition also changes. This joint contrast is an empirical constraint for the original seasonal seed-fate question, not evidence of a new universal resource mechanism. Do not reduce it to a resource-ratio model.
