# Wyoming external cross-scale replication — design lock v3\n\nDate: 2026-09-29\n\nStatus: frozen before Wyoming source schema or species-specific support counts are opened.\n\n## Why v3 changes the primary population endpoint\n\nThe Wyoming trapping design deliberately varies trap type and bait, and those choices materially affect detection. The published study also notes that detectability can differ among individuals of the same species, including by sex. Therefore a uniform-trap spatial null could transform detector preference into an apparent sex spatial effect.\n\nPrimary one-night endpoint is now **conditional sex-space Z**.\n\nFor each focal species × site × night:\n1. retain the pooled known-sex captured individuals and their capture locations;\n2. calculate observed `MPD_male - MPD_female`;\n3. define detector stratum = `trap_type × bait_type`;\n4. preserve the observed male count within every detector stratum;\n5. permute sex labels only within strata;\n6. standardize the observed contrast by the conditional null mean and SD.\n\nSmall permutation spaces are enumerated exactly (<=200,000 assignments). Larger spaces use 19,999 deterministic Monte Carlo assignments with a content-derived seed.\n\nThis null conditions on the realized captured population and detector strata. It asks whether **sex is associated with spatial configuration beyond the trap/bait capture structure**.\n\n## Legacy compatibility\n\nThe prior geometry-conditioned `Packing_z_male - Packing_z_female` remains a secondary compatibility endpoint only. It cannot determine the confirmatory Wyoming decision.\n\n## Species roles\n\n- confirmatory independent external replication: *Dipodomys ordii*;\n- fully held-out extension: *Perognathus fasciatus*.\n\nThe held-out extension cannot rescue failure of the confirmatory species.\n\n## Thresholds and aggregation\n\nPrimary inclusion remains N_male>=3 and N_female>=3; N>=5 is sensitivity only.\n\nEligible nights/events are averaged within site; sites are equally weighted. Final uncertainty uses Student-t intervals over independent site means.\n\nMinimum estimability remains >=5 overlapping sites, with >=10 eligible packing nights and movement support in the same external data.\n\n## Cross-scale interpretation\n\nMovement confirmed positive + packing 95% CI wholly within [-0.5,+0.5] Z -> `decoupled`.\n\nMovement confirmed positive + packing lower 95% CI >0 -> `coupled_positive`.\n\nMovement confirmed positive + packing upper 95% CI <0 -> `opposed`.\n\nAll other combinations -> `unresolved`.\n\nThe design does not privilege decoupling: replication of positive packing is an explicitly allowed result.\n\n## Effect status at lock\n\n- source schema inspected: false\n- species support counts inspected: false\n- sex-specific effects inspected: false\n- movement distances inspected: false\n- packing effects inspected: false\n- ecological effect models fit: 0\n
