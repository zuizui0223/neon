## Actual observed terminal counts, not projected from seed rain
All 56 one-square-metre experimental plots, 7 replicates per forest × exclusion × density-distance context. All terminal alive records have STAGE=seedling. Each context contained 35 or 350 supplied seeds. Complete denominators verified. SURV=0 means survived to end of study per provider.

In three of four observed contexts the per-seed terminal living-seedling fraction is lower in the high-density/far-distance context while absolute recorded living seedling counts per plot are higher. In unlogged/open both increase slightly in probability and strongly in number. Ratios obey exact count identity multiplier=10×per-seed probability ratio. These are actual endpoint counts rather than area projections from unlinked natural seed rain. The observations are correlated within source trees and treatments; not four independent studies, no CI or new significance test.

## Timing and causal boundaries
All blocks structurally matched by source tree × density × start date (24 blocks across 12 source trees). Only 16 passed common terminal censor-date gate (32–53d duration), 8 had absent/nonunique censor dates. Paired timing STOP retained; no tree-weighted treatment or density-distance effects computed, no subgroup rescue. Descriptive pooled context counts may reflect varying duration, repeated trees, forest type and parent-distance effects. Density 5 vs 50 fixed at 2m vs 32m, not an independent density manipulation. Exclusion includes all vertebrates and may involve cage effects; do not attribute the difference to rodents, particular animals, seed transport or causality. One 2014 mast event, no seasonal replication. The published study already analyzed recruitment and mortality, and the rate/count principle is established in Wright et al. 2005 DOI 10.1890/03-0750. New work here is the explicit common-unit terminal count comparison and strict support boundaries. No claim of new broad ecological law, no formal meta-analysis.

## Editorial decision
V18 conditional area projection remains hypothetical. V19 adds direct observed final living-seedling counts in a seed-addition/exclusion experiment, but does not connect them to natural dispersal. The following genuinely distinct target is repeated natural seed cohorts with measured emergence, comparable effort/horizon, and independent resource or consumer context.
