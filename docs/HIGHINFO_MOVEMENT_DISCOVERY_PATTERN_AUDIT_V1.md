# Discovery-pattern audit for the unseen-species validation

Date: 2026-09-29

Status: post hoc characterization of the stopped discovery dataset, recorded before any movement outcome is inspected in the validation species.

## What generated the validation hypothesis

In the stopped three-species programme, the pre-specified N>=5/sex sensitivity had a positive family effect with a 95% CI above zero and very similar species effects.

## What the discovery data do NOT show

An exploratory event-level audit does not show a strong monotonic increase of the male-minus-female movement contrast with the minimum sex-specific recapture count.

Pearson correlations between min(N_male, N_female) and event Delta_movement among N>=2/sex events were approximately:
- Chaetodipus penicillatus: +0.076
- Dipodomys merriami: +0.028
- Dipodomys ordii: +0.103

Therefore the new validation must not be interpreted as a test that density, sample size, or recapture abundance causally increases sex-biased movement.

## Allowed confirmatory question

The validation tests only whether the **specific high-information event condition already observed in discovery (N>=5 per sex)** reproduces a positive male-minus-female movement contrast in outcome-uninspected heteromyid species.

If validation succeeds, the result supports replication of that threshold-defined pattern. Mechanisms involving density, breeding state, detectability, or estimator reliability would remain separate future questions.

If validation fails, the N>=5 discovery pattern remains a non-generalizing sensitivity result.
