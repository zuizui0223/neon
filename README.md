## Sampling and scientific limits

The original source-support denominator was 1,838 effort-valid selected 3-night bouts and 803 adjacent-season candidate pairs. Only 58 pairs (14 sites) passed the ≥3 common genus / season-exclusive tagged-animal primary gate; 216 pairs met the pre-existing diagnostic ≥2-genus rule, **not an independently positive ecological effect**. Sites without qualifying pairs are missing *estimands*, not sites with demonstrated zero recurrence. Avoid extrapolating a genus-level 14-site spatial signature across every site, biome, rodent species or full home range.

The current CHM habitat exposure remains blocked by unavailable historically aligned all-trap locations and source-coordinate uncertainty; a 2026-10-22 GraphQL/REST cross-source comparison stopped at a 5-station preflight. This blocks only station-scale habitat mechanism fitting, **not** the integrated ecological research design or site-level climate source feasibility.

The historical pooled density slopes had beta_W = -4.395, beta_B = +4.977, and delta = +9.372 m²/MNKA. Those signs do **NOT** resurrect the failed >=6/8 genus criterion. Future taxon/trait/density moderators are subject to a new independent data and lock requirement, not a post-result way to create positive significance.
